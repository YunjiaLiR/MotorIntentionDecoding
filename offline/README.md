# Offline EEG analysis

Exploratory EEG analysis and decoding research scripts, grouped by purpose.

- `preprocessing/`: loading/cleaning and data preparation.
- `classification/`: motor-imagery classification including CSP/FBCSP and hierarchical variants.
- `visualization/`: spectral, time–frequency and spatial visualisations.

Scripts preserve their original research names and assumptions. They are not a unified executable package. No raw participant recordings are included.
